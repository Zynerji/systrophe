**Personalisation notes**:
- Each aerospace prime has its own "advanced concepts" or "future capabilities" group. Find the specific group before emailing.
- Tone: peer-grade engineering, not popular science.
- Do NOT promise FTL space travel. Emphasise: analytical framework, hardware-validated mathematics, open-source IP, patent-protected.
- Expect 50%+ of emails to receive no response. The 1-2 that do may convert to high-value engagements.
- Avoid the term "warp drive" in the subject line if possible; use "speculative propulsion concept" instead to slip past corporate-email-filter biases.
