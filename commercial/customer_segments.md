# Knopp Drive — customer segments

A market analysis of who would license, partner on, or invest in the Knopp Drive IP. Ordered by near-term relevance.

---

## Tier 1 — High near-term interest (12-24 month horizon)

### 1. Aerospace primes (R&D divisions)

**Targets**: Lockheed Martin Skunk Works, Boeing Phantom Works, Northrop Grumman, Airbus Defence and Space, MBDA, Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Defence Systems.

**Why they care**: These organisations maintain dedicated "future-of-flight" / advanced-concepts groups that pursue speculative-but-rigorous propulsion R&D on long horizons. The Knopp Drive's strongest selling point — "zero exotic matter inside CTC band" — converts what was a permanent disqualifier (infinite negative energy) into a finite engineering budget. Even partial credibility of that claim is sufficient to fund a several-million-dollar concept study.

**Engagement model**: white-paper request → exploratory phone call → SOW for a 12-month feasibility study. Typical study budget: $1-5M.

**Decision-maker title**: VP, Advanced Programs / VP, Future Concepts.

### 2. Space agencies (advanced-concepts offices)

**Targets**:
- NASA Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC) — `https://www.nasa.gov/niac/`
- ESA Future Concepts (Discovery, GSP)
- JAXA Space Exploration Innovation Hub Center
- CNSA Long March Advanced R&D

**Why they care**: NIAC explicitly funds "early-stage" propulsion concepts at the 10-30 year horizon. The Knopp Drive's open-source code + hardware-confirmed amplitude structure is exactly the level of maturity NIAC prefers (TRL 1-2 with credible analytical foundation).

**Engagement model**: NIAC Phase I proposal (~$175K), then Phase II ($600K), then Phase III ($2M).

**Decision-maker title**: NIAC Program Executive.

### 3. Quantum computing companies (basic science divisions)

**Targets**: IBM Quantum, Quantinuum, IonQ, Pasqal, Rigetti.

**Why they care**: The Knopp Drive's hardware-confirmation experiment is a demonstration of practical quantum advantage on a non-trivial physics problem. IBM Quantum has already provided the platform for batch 6; they may be interested in featuring the Knopp Drive as a case study for their "Quantum Network" customer-engagement program. Competitors will want to reproduce on their own platforms.

**Engagement model**: research collaboration → joint case study → IBM/Quantinuum-co-authored follow-up paper.

**Decision-maker title**: VP Research, Quantum Computing.

## Tier 2 — Medium near-term interest (24-48 month horizon)

### 4. Defence research agencies

**Targets**:
- DARPA (Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency, US)
- DARO (Defense Advanced Research Office, JP)
- DSTL (Defence Science and Technology Laboratory, UK)
- DLR (Deutsches Zentrum für Luft- und Raumfahrt, DE)
- KARI (Korea Aerospace Research Institute)

**Why they care**: Long-horizon propulsion R&D is a core DARPA mission (cf. Project Pegasus, Project Greenglow). The dual-use implications of any FTL-capable propulsion are obvious. Critical caveat: the Knopp Drive's chronology-protection ambiguity (the CTC band carries genuine causal-paradox potential) means defence engagement should be approached with strong export-control awareness.

**Engagement model**: SBIR/STTR (US), Innovate UK / KETs (UK), MoD France PEC. Typical award: $500K–$2M.

**Decision-maker title**: Program Manager.

### 5. Fusion / advanced-energy startups

**Targets**: Commonwealth Fusion Systems, Helion Energy, TAE Technologies, First Light Fusion, ZAP Energy, General Fusion.

**Why they care**: The Q-cavity feedback amplification mechanism (sustained drive power $\sim 1/Q^2$ from a parametric resonator in a superconducting/cryogenic cavity) is structurally similar to the cavity engineering already done in stellarator/tokamak / magneto-inertial fusion. Cross-licensing of the cavity engineering may be of interest.

**Engagement model**: cross-IP licensing discussion.

**Decision-maker title**: CTO or Chief Scientist.

## Tier 3 — Long-horizon strategic interest (48+ month horizon)

### 6. Academic research consortia

**Targets**:
- Caltech (TAPIR group)
- MIT Center for Theoretical Physics
- Perimeter Institute
- Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (Potsdam)
- Cambridge DAMTP
- Stanford / SLAC
- Princeton / IAS

**Why they care**: Several of the Knopp Drive's components (Bobrick--Martire shells, Lentz solitons, Krasnikov tubes) have active academic research programs. Citations and follow-up work from these groups would establish the Knopp Drive as a recognised construction in the literature.

**Engagement model**: open-source code + arXiv preprint → naturally drives citations. Optional invited seminar series.

### 7. Private space companies

**Targets**: SpaceX, Blue Origin, Sierra Space, Rocket Lab, Stoke Space.

**Why they care**: These companies are focused on chemical / nuclear propulsion in the near term and have limited interest in TRL 1-2 concepts. However, SpaceX in particular has a long-horizon Mars-colonisation roadmap that could eventually find value in the Earth–Mars zero-exotic-matter result.

**Engagement model**: defer until Phase 4 (commercial pilot).

### 8. Sci-fi / popular-science partnerships

**Targets**: Discovery / Science Channel, PBS NOVA, Veritasium, PBS Space Time, Kurzgesagt, Up and Atom.

**Why they care**: The Knopp Drive is a marketable popular-science story. Hardware confirmation on IBM Marrakesh + zero-exotic-matter Earth--Mars is the headline.

**Engagement model**: press kit + interview availability.

## Tier 4 — Specialty / niche

### 9. Foundation funders

**Targets**: Breakthrough Initiatives (Yuri Milner), Templeton Foundation, Buchalter Foundation, FQXi (Foundational Questions Institute).

**Why they care**: Foundation funding is the natural source for high-risk speculative-but-rigorous physics.

**Engagement model**: grant application. Typical award: $50K-$500K.

### 10. Insurance / risk-modelling for futurist scenarios

**Targets**: Lloyd's of London emerging-risks practice, Munich Re innovation lab.

**Why they care**: If any of the speculative-propulsion sector matures, insurance modelling will be required.

**Engagement model**: defer until Phase 4.

---

## Anti-targets

- **Cryptocurrency / Web3 projects**: high credibility risk; we should not engage.
- **Vacuum-energy / free-energy / overunity fringe**: same.
- **Defence agencies of adversarial states**: export-control violations.
