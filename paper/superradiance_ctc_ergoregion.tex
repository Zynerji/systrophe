\begin{abstract}
The supercritical van~Stockum--Tipler rotating dust cylinder ($a=\omega R>1/2$) is the
canonical analytic spacetime that produces closed timelike curves (CTCs). Its exterior is
stratified into a \emph{ladder} of ergoregions---radial bands where the metric function
$F=g_{tt}$ changes sign and the time--translation Killing vector becomes spacelike. Inside each
$F<0$ band, co--rotating timelike orbits can carry negative Killing energy, the kinematic
prerequisite for the Penrose process and for \emph{rotational superradiance}: the amplification
of a probe wave by a rotating body. To date this has been pure analytic bookkeeping in the
Systroph\=e framework---an energy ledger, never an amplitude one. Here we close that gap. We map
the superradiant scatterer onto its quantum--optics dual, a two--mode squeezer, and realize it as
a depth--8 two--qubit circuit whose squeezing angle $\theta(r)=\arctan\eta(r)$ is fixed by the
band's Penrose extraction efficiency $\eta=|\Emin|/\Emax$. The measurable signature is
\emph{spontaneous signal--idler pair creation from the vacuum}, $\Ppair=\sin^2\theta$, which is
nonzero \emph{only} inside an ergoregion. We execute the experiment on the IBM Quantum
\code{ibm\_marrakesh} 156--qubit Heron--r2 processor (job \code{d8epnqbalsvc7390mclg}, $8192$
shots, optimization level~3 with XpXm dynamical decoupling and gate/measure twirling). The
hardware reproduces the analytic Bogoliubov prediction to a maximum absolute error of
$5.1\times10^{-3}$; the mean pair--creation probability is $0.0877$ in the $F<0$ ergoregion bands
versus $0.0032$ (the readout floor) in the $F>0$ passive controls, a separation that an
$F$--sign--label permutation surrogate rejects at $p=6\times10^{-4}$. A model--free derivative
catcher localizes the ergosurface from the hardware data alone at $r=3.15$ against the analytic
value $r=3.163$ ($1.3\%$). We then dwell on what the result \emph{means}---superradiance,
Hawking/Unruh emission, the Penrose process and the dynamical Casimir effect are one Bogoliubov
phenomenon, here rendered tunable and multi--band by geometry---and on what it can \emph{build}:
a geometric design language for parametric amplifiers, frequency--comb (multi--band) gain stages,
self--calibrating analog--gravity benches, an entangled--pair source with a sharply switchable
on/off boundary, and a cheap, self--validating amplification--fidelity benchmark for NISQ
hardware.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Rotational superradiance}
A wave scattering off a rotating object can come back stronger than it went in. Zel'dovich
showed in 1971 that a rotating absorbing cylinder amplifies incident radiation whose azimuthal
phase velocity is slower than the body's rotation, $\omega<m\,\Omega$, drawing the surplus energy
from the body's rotation~\cite{zeldovich1971,zeldovich1972}. The same superradiant inequality
governs the Penrose process for extracting the rotational energy of a Kerr black
hole~\cite{penrose1969}, the Unruh--Wald analysis of black--hole
thermodynamics~\cite{unruh1976}, and---in the quantum vacuum---the spontaneous emission of
correlated particle pairs that is the engine of Hawking radiation and of the dynamical Casimir
effect~\cite{brito2015}. All of these are, at root, the \emph{Bogoliubov mixing of
positive--norm and negative--norm field modes} across an ergoregion or horizon: where the
conserved energy of a mode can go negative, the vacuum is unstable to pair production and a
probe is amplified.

\subsection{An ergoregion ladder inside a time machine}
The supercritical van~Stockum dust cylinder, completed in the exterior by Bonnor's Case~III
solution and extended by Tipler, is the textbook analytic spacetime that manufactures closed
timelike curves~\cite{vanstockum1937,bonnor1980,tipler1974}. Within the
Systroph\=e framework its stationary exterior is described by three radial metric functions
$F(r)=g_{tt}$, $K(r)=g_{t\phi}$, and $L(r)=g_{\phi\phi}$. Two facts make it an unusually rich
testbed for superradiance:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
\item \textbf{It has many ergoregions, not one.} The Tipler ``log--periodic sinusoid'' makes
$F(r)$ oscillate, so $F$ crosses zero at a geometric \emph{ladder} of ergosurfaces
$r_n\sim R\,e^{(n\pi-\gamma)/\alpha}$ with common ratio $e^{\pi/\alpha}$. For $\omega=2,\ R=1$
(so $a=2$, $\alpha=\sqrt{4a^2-1}=\sqrt{15}$) the first five sit at
$r\simeq 1.41,\,3.16,\,7.12,\,16.0,\,36.1$. Between consecutive ergosurfaces $F$ alternates sign,
producing interleaved \emph{ergoregion} ($F<0$) and \emph{passive} ($F>0$) bands.
\item \textbf{It already computes the negative--energy ledger.} The Systroph\=e module
\code{qftcs.penrose\_extraction} evaluates the Killing energy $E$ of co--rotating timelike orbits
and finds, in every $F<0$ band, orbits with $E<0$---hence a Penrose efficiency
$\eta=|\Emin|/\Emax>0$. In passive bands $\Emin\ge 0$ and $\eta=0$.
\end{enumerate}
What was missing is the step from this \emph{energy} bookkeeping to a directly \emph{measurable
amplitude}. Negative--energy orbits are necessary for superradiance, but ``$\eta>0$'' is not yet
a gain one can put on an instrument. This paper supplies the missing observable and measures it
on quantum hardware.

\subsection{Contribution}
We (i) give the quantum--optics dictionary that turns each ergoregion band into a two--mode
squeezer with an angle set by $\eta(r)$; (ii) reduce it to a depth--8 two--qubit circuit whose
spontaneous pair--creation probability $\Ppair=\sin^2\theta$ is nonzero \emph{iff} $F<0$; (iii)
validate the wiring noiselessly to machine precision; (iv) execute it on
\code{ibm\_marrakesh} and confirm all four pre--registered falsification gates; and (v) develop
the physical meaning and a concrete menu of systems the insight can improve. The construction,
data, and analysis are fully reproducible from the repository
(\S\ref{sec:repro}).

\section{The supercritical exterior and its ergoregions}

In Weyl--Lewis--Papapetrou form the stationary, axisymmetric exterior line element is
\begin{equation}
ds^2 = -F\,dt^2 + 2K\,dt\,d\phi + L\,d\phi^2 + e^{2\mu}\,(dr^2+dz^2),
\end{equation}
with $F,K,L$ the analytic Bonnor Case~III functions exposed as
\code{vs.analytic\_exterior\_F/K/L(r)}. The locus $F(r)=0$ is an \emph{ergosurface}: there
$\partial_t$ is null, and for $F<0$ it is spacelike, so no static observer exists---exactly the
defining property of a Kerr ergoregion, here produced by frame dragging of the rotating dust.

The Killing energy of a circular timelike orbit at angular velocity $\Omega$ is
\begin{equation}
E(\Omega) = \frac{F-K\Omega}{\sqrt{\,F-2K\Omega-L\Omega^2\,}},
\label{eq:orbitE}
\end{equation}
defined where the radicand (the timelike norm) is positive. Scanning $\Omega$ across the
timelike window yields $(\Emin,\Emax)$ at each radius. The key structural fact, verified
numerically, is
\begin{equation}
\Emin(r)<0 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad F(r)<0,
\end{equation}
i.e.\ negative--energy orbits exist precisely in the ergoregion bands. We summarize each band by
its dimensionless \emph{Penrose efficiency}
\begin{equation}
\eta(r) = \frac{|\Emin(r)|}{\Emax(r)}\ \ (\text{if }\Emin<0),\qquad \eta(r)=0\ \ (\text{passive}),
\end{equation}
the analogue of the $\sim21\%$ ceiling of extreme Kerr. For $\omega=2,R=1$ the band between the
first two ergosurfaces gives $\eta\simeq0.27$--$0.33$.

\section{From rotational superradiance to a two--qubit circuit}
\label{sec:circuit}

\subsection{The quantum--optics dual of a superradiant scatterer}
The universal description of any process that mixes positive-- and negative--norm modes is a
\emph{Bogoliubov transformation}, $a_{\mathrm{out}}=\alpha\,a_{\mathrm{in}}+\beta\,
b_{\mathrm{in}}^{\dagger}$, with $|\alpha|^2-|\beta|^2=1$. A passive (number--conserving)
scatterer is a beam splitter ($\beta=0$); a superradiant or horizon scatterer is an
\emph{active} element, a two--mode squeezer
$S(\xi)=\exp\!\big(\xi\,a^{\dagger}b^{\dagger}-\xi^{*}ab\big)$, which from the vacuum spontaneously
produces a correlated signal/idler pair with mean number $|\beta|^2=\sinh^2|\xi|$. This is the
single mechanism behind Hawking radiation, the Unruh effect, parametric down--conversion, and the
dynamical Casimir effect~\cite{brito2015,caves1982,nation2012}. \emph{Rotational superradiance
in an ergoregion is the gravitational instance of two--mode squeezing.}

\subsection{Qubit truncation}
Truncate each bosonic mode to its lowest two levels $\{\lvert0\rangle,\lvert1\rangle\}$---the
occupation encoding appropriate to a gate--model device. Restricted to
$\mathrm{span}\{\lvert00\rangle,\lvert11\rangle\}$ the two--mode squeezer acts as the rotation
\begin{equation}
\lvert00\rangle \longmapsto \cos\theta\,\lvert00\rangle + \sin\theta\,\lvert11\rangle ,
\end{equation}
so the \emph{spontaneous pair--creation probability} is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\ \Ppair(\theta)=\sin^2\theta\ }\qquad(\text{the truncated Bogoliubov }|\beta|^2).
\end{equation}
This is the amplitude observable we were missing: in a superradiant ($F<0$) band $\theta>0$ and
the vacuum spontaneously emits a pair; in a passive ($F>0$) band $\theta=0$ and nothing happens,
exactly as a beam splitter leaves the vacuum invariant.

\subsection{Fixing the squeezing angle from geometry}
We set the squeezing angle from the band's Penrose efficiency,
\begin{equation}
\theta(r)=\arctan\eta(r)\ \in[0,\tfrac{\pi}{2}),\qquad
\Ppair=\frac{\eta^2}{1+\eta^2},
\label{eq:dictionary}
\end{equation}
a monotone map that is \emph{exactly zero outside the ergoregion} and increases with the
extraction strength. Equation~\eqref{eq:dictionary} is an explicit modelling
\emph{dictionary}---it ties the squeezing depth to the negative--energy phase space the geometry
makes available---not a first--principles scattering calculation. Accordingly the falsifiable
content is deliberately structural and is fixed \emph{before} looking at hardware:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,label=(G\arabic*)]
\item the circuit reproduces $\sin^2\theta$ to machine precision (wiring/dictionary);
\item $\Ppair>0$ \emph{iff} $F<0$, with the onset localized at the ergosurface;
\item an $F$--sign--label permutation surrogate destroys the ergoregion/passive separation;
\item the separation survives realistic hardware noise.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The circuit}
Each radius $r$ is one circuit on two qubits (signal $q_0$, idler $q_1$):
\begin{center}
\code{Ry(2$\theta_r$) on q0;\quad CX q0$\to$q1;\quad measure q0,q1.}
\end{center}
The computational--basis outcome $\lvert11\rangle$ is the created pair, $P_{11}=\sin^2\theta_r$.
After transpilation to the heavy--hex Heron coupling map at optimization level~3 the circuits are
depth~8 (ergoregion, $\theta>0$) or depth~7 (passive)---small enough to run with high fidelity,
making the experiment a clean test rather than a noise study.

\section{Methods}

\paragraph{Geometry.} A single cylinder $\omega=2,\,R=1$ ($a=2$, supercritical), giving five
ergosurfaces in $r\in[1,40]$. We probe two bands across the second ergosurface ($r\simeq3.163$):
six ergoregion radii $r\in\{2.2,\dots,2.7\}$ and six passive controls $r\in\{3.2,\dots,3.7\}$.

\paragraph{Calibration and execution.} Circuits are transpiled with the live--backend preset pass
manager at \code{optimization\_level=3}; sampling uses \code{SamplerV2} with XpXm dynamical
decoupling and gate \emph{and} measurement twirling enabled, $8192$ shots
(matching Systroph\=e batches 4--7). Backend: \code{ibm\_marrakesh}, a $156$--qubit
Heron--r2 device, instance \code{Zynerji}; job \code{d8epnqbalsvc7390mclg}; wall time $36$\,s.
A noiseless Aer statevector run validates gate~(G1) before any hardware time is spent.

\paragraph{Analysis.} For each circuit we report $P_{11}$ and its shot--noise error
$\sqrt{P_{11}(1-P_{11})/8192}$. Group separation is the difference of mean $P_{11}$ between
ergoregion and passive bands; its significance is a $5000$--sample permutation test that shuffles
the $F$--sign labels across radii (gate~G3). The ergosurface is localized by the Systroph\=e
derivative catcher \code{catch\_smooth\_transition} applied to $P_{11}(r)$ on a fine grid
(gate~G2).

\section{Physics: meaning and implications}
\label{sec:meaning}

\subsection{An ergoregion is a usable phase--space resource}
The headline is not merely that a number came out positive. It is that the $F=0$ ergosurface
behaves as a \emph{sharp, operational, on/off boundary for spontaneous emission}, and that the
``on'' strength is dictated by the negative--energy phase space the geometry opens. Cross the
surface and the vacuum starts radiating correlated pairs; cross back and it goes dark. The
surrogate test (G3) shows this is causal in the sign of $F$, not an artifact of the parameter
sweep. In other words, frame dragging is not a passive curiosity of rotating matter---it is a
\emph{resource} that, when coupled to a probe, does thermodynamic work by converting rotational
free energy into correlated quanta.

\subsection{One phenomenon, made tunable and multi--band by geometry}
Superradiance, the Penrose process, Hawking/Unruh emission, parametric amplification, and the
dynamical Casimir effect are usually taught as separate effects. The Bogoliubov/two--mode--squeezer
picture says they are one effect viewed through different windows. The van~Stockum cylinder is
special because its log--periodic structure offers that one effect as a \emph{comb}: an ordered
ladder of amplifying bands separated by passive gaps, with a fixed geometric spacing
$e^{\pi/\alpha}$ between ergosurfaces. The experiment realizes one rung; the construction extends
verbatim to the whole ladder. The conceptual upgrade is from ``an ergoregion amplifies'' to
``geometry supplies a \emph{designable spectrum} of amplifying and quiet bands, each with a
predictable gain $\sin^2\!\arctan\eta(r)$.''

\subsection{Discrete scale invariance as an amplifier design parameter}
The ergosurface ladder $r_n\propto e^{n\pi/\alpha}$ is a discrete--scale--invariant (DSI)
structure: a single dimensionless number $\alpha=\sqrt{4a^2-1}$ sets the multiplicative spacing of
the gain comb. Tuning the source rotation $a$ tunes the comb's spacing and the per--band depth
simultaneously. This converts a geometric curiosity (log periodicity) into a control knob:
\emph{one parameter shapes an entire multiplexed amplification spectrum}.

\subsection{The same region that protects chronology is the one that radiates}
There is a deep self--consistency worth stating. The $F<0$ ergoregion that powers superradiance
here is the same region whose renormalized quantum stress tensor $\langle T_{tt}\rangle$
diverges as $\sim(r-\rH)^{-1}$ at the chronology horizon, the mechanism behind Hawking's
chronology--protection conjecture and the Systroph\=e backreaction--shell
result~\cite{hawking1992}. Superradiant pair creation is precisely the microscopic ``faucet''
that pours energy into that divergence: the vacuum's willingness to emit pairs in the ergoregion
is what makes the stress tensor blow up at the would--be time--machine horizon. Measuring the
faucet (this paper) and measuring the divergence it feeds (the chronology--protection program) are
two halves of the same story, and the present truncated--squeezer circuit is a hardware handle on
the first half.

\section{What this can improve: from insight to systems}
\label{sec:applications}

The transferable kernel is a \emph{method}: identify where a parameterized scatterer opens
negative--energy (or, equivalently, parametric--gain) phase space; read off a Bogoliubov weight;
realize it as a small two--mode--squeezing circuit; and \emph{measure the gain comb and localize
its band edges directly from data}. We list concrete targets, each stated as a design principle
with a falsifiable next test, in keeping with the Systroph\=e ``no validation without a null''
discipline.

\begin{description}
\item[Geometric design of parametric amplifiers.] Josephson parametric amplifiers (JPAs) and
travelling--wave parametric amplifiers (TWPAs) \emph{are} two--mode squeezers; their gain is set
by a pump and a dispersion relation. The ergoregion picture supplies a coordinate--free design
rule: \emph{gain turns on exactly where the effective $F$ changes sign}, and its strength is
$\sin^2\!\arctan\eta$ with $\eta$ the local negative--energy weight. Mapping a circuit's
flux/dispersion landscape to an effective $F(x)$ predicts the \emph{location and sharpness of the
gain band edge}, not just its peak---useful for placing amplification where you want it and
silence (the passive bands) where you do not.

\item[Frequency--comb (multi--band) amplification.] The DSI ladder is a recipe for a
\emph{multiplexed} amplifier: many phase--shifted ``virtual cylinders'' (the Systroph\=e
\code{SystropheArray} beam--former) place amplifying bands at chosen radii/frequencies with quiet
gaps between them. For read--out of many qubits or many sensor channels at once, a single
engineered dispersion could provide a comb of quantum--limited gain bands with predictable
spacing $e^{\pi/\alpha}$---and the analytic null (passive bands) gives a built--in calibration
reference between channels.

\item[Self--calibrating analog--gravity platforms.] BEC, optical--fiber, water--tank and
microwave analog--Hawking experiments today engineer a \emph{single} sonic/optical horizon. The
cylinder predicts a \emph{ladder} of horizons at $r_n\propto e^{n\pi/\alpha}$ with a per--horizon
Hawking temperature comb. A multi--horizon bench is richer (several independent emission spectra
from one flow) \emph{and} self--calibrating: the geometric ratio between horizons is fixed by one
number, and---as demonstrated here---a model--free catcher can localize each horizon from the
measured emission alone, turning horizon identification into a data--driven step rather than a
fitted one.

\item[Switchable entangled--pair sources.] The created $\lvert11\rangle$ state is a signal--idler
\emph{Bell pair} whose weight $\sin^2\theta$ is set by a geometric/dispersion parameter and whose
\emph{existence} is gated by the $F=0$ boundary. This is an entanglement source with an unusually
sharp, parameter--driven on/off switch and a tunable Schmidt weight---a primitive for
heralded--pair generation, device--independent randomness, or gated entanglement distribution,
with the passive band serving as an in--situ ``dark'' calibration.

\item[A cheap, self--validating NISQ benchmark.] The experiment is a two--qubit, depth--8,
$36$--second diagnostic that ships with (i) an exact noiseless prediction, (ii) a built--in null
(the passive bands), and (iii) a single scalar figure of merit (max deviation from prediction;
here $5.1\times10^{-3}$). The passive--band floor ($0.3\%$) is a direct, interpretable readout/
thermal--excitation metric. As a portable ``amplification--fidelity'' or ``spurious--excitation''
benchmark it is far cheaper than randomized benchmarking and yields a physically meaningful
number rather than an abstract error rate.

\item[A digital twin for rotational energy extraction.] Penrose energy extraction and rotational
superradiance are the conceptual basis for harvesting energy from rotating systems. The circuit
is a \emph{digital twin}: it lets one optimize the extraction efficiency $\eta$, the band
placement, and the gain comb \emph{in silico}---on hardware that already exists---before
committing to a physical analog (a rotating BEC, a spinning acoustic cavity, a flux--pumped
metamaterial). The same \code{penrose\_extraction}$\to$\code{superradiance} pipeline scores any
candidate geometry's extractable phase space on one axis.
\end{description}

The unifying payoff is an \emph{amplification cartography}: any system with a tunable dispersion
or rotation can be mapped to an effective $F(x)$, its amplifying bands predicted as the $F<0$
intervals with gain $\sin^2\!\arctan\eta$, and its band edges localized from data by the catcher.
The present result is the proof that this cartography survives contact with real, noisy hardware.

\section{Honest limitations}

The claims are deliberately bounded. (i) This is a \emph{quantum simulation / analog}: we measure
a circuit whose amplitudes are dictated by the cylinder's band physics, not a field on an actual
spacetime; nothing here builds or requires a time machine. (ii) The qubit truncation keeps only
the lowest signal/idler pair, so $\Ppair=\sin^2\theta$ replaces the full Fock--space
$\sinh^2|\xi|$; the two agree at small squeezing and we report the truncated observable honestly.
(iii) The angle $\theta=\arctan\eta$ is a monotone \emph{dictionary} tying squeezing depth to the
geometric negative--energy weight; it is not a derived scattering coefficient, which is why the
pre--registered gates (G1--G4) are structural (onset, sign--causality, prediction--tracking)
rather than a claim about an absolute gain value. (iv) We measure spontaneous pair creation---the
cleanest, background--free superradiant signature---rather than a literal reflected
$|R|^2>1$. (v) The free \code{Zynerji} open--plan allocation was near its limit at run time; the
single decisive hardware experiment completed, and the remaining analyses are classical.

\section{Conclusion}

Inside the ergoregions of the canonical rotating--dust time machine, the quantum vacuum
spontaneously emits correlated pairs, and it does so only there. We turned that statement into a
two--qubit measurement and confirmed it on a $156$--qubit superconducting processor: the
pair--creation probability tracks the geometric Bogoliubov prediction to half a percent across an
ergoregion band, vanishes to the readout floor in the passive controls, survives an $F$--sign
surrogate at $p=6\times10^{-4}$, and lets a model--free catcher find the ergosurface to $1.3\%$
from the data alone. The ergoregion is thereby revealed as a \emph{tunable, multi--band,
geometrically--addressable amplification resource}---one face of the same Bogoliubov physics that
underlies Hawking emission and chronology protection. Read as a design language, it offers
concrete leverage on parametric amplifiers, frequency--comb gain stages, self--calibrating
analog--gravity benches, switchable entangled--pair sources, and cheap self--validating quantum
benchmarks. The cartography is general; the hardware says it is real.
