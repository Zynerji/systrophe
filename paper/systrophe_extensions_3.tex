\begin{abstract}
We add five further modules to the Systroph\=e framework
\cite{Knopp2026Systrophe,Knopp2026QFTCS,Knopp2026Ext1,Knopp2026Ext2}
covering boundary, cosmological, and observational aspects of the
Tipler / Lewis-Papapetrou structure: (1) a speculative AdS/CFT-style
dictionary mapping the supercritical exterior to a non-unitary CFT
with complex conformal weights $\Delta = 1 \pm i\alpha/2$;
(2) quantum-information channel capacities for radial transport in
the LP exterior, including Deutsch and Lloyd CTC channel models;
(3) cosmological embedding of the cylinder in an FRW background,
yielding a Hubble-induced "evaporation" of CTC bands; (4) spinor
monodromy along closed phi-loops with explicit Spin(1,3) holonomy
matrices and a Systroph\=e-pair extinction proxy; (5) synchrotron-
analog radiation from charged orbits in CTC bands. Eighty-two new
tests across the five modules, all passing. Code at
\texttt{github.com/Zynerji/systrophe} (v0.18.0+).
\end{abstract}

\section{Holographic dictionary (Phase 16)}

The LP supercritical exterior is not asymptotically AdS, but at
large $r$ the metric components oscillate log-periodically about a
power-law envelope. Treating this as a "log-periodic CFT" boundary,
we extract effective conformal weights
\[
\Delta = 1 \pm i\,\alpha/2,
\]
which are \emph{complex}: a hallmark of non-unitary CFTs with
discrete-scale-invariant RG flow \cite{Sornette1998,Faulkner2010}.

The module \texttt{holographic.py} provides:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{boundary\_expansion\_coefficients}: extract $(\beta, \alpha, \delta)$ at large $r$;
\item \texttt{dual\_operator\_dimensions}: complex $\Delta$ from $\alpha$;
\item \texttt{brown\_york\_stress\_tensor}: quasi-local stress at finite $r$;
\item \texttt{holographic\_entropy\_strip}: Ryu-Takayanagi-style entanglement;
\item \texttt{boundary\_two\_point\_correlator}: $\langle O(s) O(0)\rangle \sim s^{-2}e^{-i\alpha\ln s}$;
\item \texttt{efimov\_correspondence}: map to 3-body Efimov scaling $\lambda_E$.
\end{itemize}

A two-point correlator phase fit recovers $\alpha$ to within $1\%$
of the theoretical value (test \texttt{test\_reconstruct\_alpha\_matches\_theoretical}).

\section{Quantum-information channel (Phase 17)}

The radial worldline in the LP exterior is treated as a noisy
quantum channel. Each chronology-horizon crossing applies an
amplitude-damping with parameter $p_{\rm CH}$; the cumulative
damping after $N$ crossings is $1 - (1 - p_{\rm CH})^N$. We adopt
the heuristic classical capacity
\[
C(p) = \max\!\left[\,0,\; 1 - h_2\!\left(\tfrac{1+\sqrt{1-p}}{2}\right)\right],
\]
with $h_2$ the binary entropy.

The module \texttt{qi\_channel.py} provides:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{redshift\_factor} and \texttt{amplitude\_damping\_p};
\item \texttt{channel\_capacity\_holevo}: classical capacity;
\item \texttt{chronology\_horizon\_decoherence}: cumulative $p$ after $N$ CHs;
\item \texttt{closed\_timelike\_loop\_capacity}: Deutsch classical / quantum
  capacities (both 0) and Lloyd P-CTC capacity (1 bit);
\item \texttt{entanglement\_swap\_protocol}: Bell pair fidelity vs.\ radius;
\item \texttt{channel\_assisted\_clock\_sync}: time-dilation residual.
\end{itemize}

Headline: the classical Deutsch capacity of a CTC channel vanishes
identically (the self-consistent fixed point erases input), but the
Lloyd P-CTC capacity gives 1 bit per qubit. This distinction is a
testable consequence of the chosen CTC model.

\section{Cosmological embedding (Phase 18)}

A cylinder embedded in an FRW background is exposed to Hubble drag.
We model the drag as an effective rotation reduction
$\omega_{\rm eff}(t) = \omega(1 - H(t)/\omega)$, which propagates to a
corrected log-frequency $\alpha_{\rm eff} = \sqrt{4 a_{\rm eff}^2 - 1}$.
For $\Lambda$-dominated FRW with sufficiently large $H_0$, $a_{\rm eff}$
drops below $1/2$ and the cylinder \emph{evaporates}: all CTCs are
extinguished.

The module \texttt{cosmological\_embedding.py} provides:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{frw\_scale\_factor} and \texttt{hubble\_parameter} for
  matter / radiation / $\Lambda$ EoS;
\item \texttt{matching\_residual}: LP vs.\ FRW mismatch at $r_{\rm match}$;
\item \texttt{cosmological\_corrections\_to\_alpha}: shifted log-frequency;
\item \texttt{horizon\_chirp\_rate}: $dr_1/dt$ for the first CH;
\item \texttt{cylinder\_evaporates\_under\_expansion}: evaporation time;
\item \texttt{inflation\_creates\_systrophe\_cylinders}: spontaneous
  creation rate during inflation, $H^4 e^{-S_E}$.
\end{itemize}

This connects the Systroph\=e framework to cosmological observability:
a primordial Systroph\=e cylinder could survive matter / radiation
domination but evaporates in late-time $\Lambda$ domination.

\section{Spinor monodromy along CTCs (Phase 19)}

A Dirac spinor parallel-transported around a closed phi-loop at
constant $t$ and $r$ in the LP exterior picks up a Spin(1,3)
holonomy
\[
U(r) = \exp\!\left[-\tfrac{\pi}{2}\,\omega^{ab}_{\phi}(r)\,\gamma_a\gamma_b\right].
\]

The module \texttt{spinor\_monodromy.py} provides:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{spin\_connection\_phi}: $\omega^{ab}_{\phi}(r)$ via finite-difference;
\item \texttt{spinor\_holonomy}: $4{\times}4$ holonomy matrix;
\item \texttt{monodromy\_eigenvalues}: spectrum of $U$;
\item \texttt{fixed\_point\_spinors}: eigenvectors at $+1$;
\item \texttt{multi\_loop\_monodromy}: $U^N$;
\item \texttt{pair\_modified\_monodromy}: $\delta$-interpolation
  between $U$ and identity (Systroph\=e-pair extinction);
\item \texttt{chronology\_horizon\_caustic}: blow-up loci of $|\omega^{ab}_\phi|$.
\end{itemize}

At $\delta = \pi$ (full pair extinction), the pair monodromy
collapses to the identity (test
\texttt{test\_pair\_modified\_monodromy\_extinction\_at\_pi}). This
echoes the classical $L_{\rm pair}$ extinction proxy of Phase 4 and
shows that the chronology-protection mechanism extends consistently
to spinor representations.

\section{Synchrotron radiation analog (Phase 20)}

A charged particle on a circular orbit in a CTC band radiates
electromagnetically. The orbit frequency $\Omega(r)$ follows from
the geodesic condition $F' + 2\Omega K' + \Omega^2 L' = 0$; the
effective Lorentz factor is $\gamma^{-2} = F - 2\Omega K - \Omega^2 L$;
the synchrotron critical frequency is $\omega_c = \tfrac{3}{2}\gamma^3\Omega$.

The module \texttt{synchrotron\_analog.py} provides:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{orbital\_frequency}, \texttt{effective\_gamma\_factor};
\item \texttt{synchrotron\_critical\_frequency};
\item \texttt{emitted\_power}: Larmor-like $P = (2/3)e^2 a^2 \gamma^4$;
\item \texttt{characteristic\_emission\_band};
\item \texttt{multi\_band\_synchrotron\_spectrum}: emission per CTC band;
\item \texttt{back\_reaction\_drag};
\item \texttt{observable\_signature\_distant\_observer}: redshift to far observer.
\end{itemize}

Headline: each CTC band emits a synchrotron-like spectrum with
peak at $\omega_c$ set by $r$. A distant observer sees a series of
discrete log-periodically-spaced emission bands, redshifted by
$\sqrt{F_{\rm emit}/F_{\rm obs}}$. This is a direct astrophysical
signature -- if a Systroph\=e cylinder existed, its CTC bands would
glow.

\section{Implications}

The five modules collectively extend the Systroph\=e framework into:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Holographic / RG-cycle territory}: the supercritical
  exterior is a concrete realization of a log-periodic CFT with
  complex conformal weights.
\item \textbf{Quantum-information physics}: chronology horizons
  function as amplitude-damping channels, with Deutsch and Lloyd CTC
  models giving distinct testable capacities.
\item \textbf{Cosmological context}: cylinders evaporate under $\Lambda$
  expansion; inflation creates them at rate $H^4 e^{-S_E}$.
\item \textbf{Spinorial physics}: explicit $4{\times}4$ holonomy
  matrices for parallel transport; pair extinction extends to
  spinor representations.
\item \textbf{Observational astrophysics}: synchrotron-like emission
  from CTC bands gives a redshifted log-periodic emission spectrum
  that a distant observer could detect.
\end{enumerate}
