\begin{abstract}
The supercritical van~Stockum--Tipler rotating dust cylinder ($a=\omega R>1/2$) admits closed
timelike curves (CTCs) and an associated chronology horizon. Hawking's chronology--protection
conjecture holds that quantum effects forbid the formation of such a horizon, the proposed
mechanism being the divergence of the renormalised stress--energy tensor of quantum fields as
the horizon is approached. We make this mechanism \emph{quantitative} and \emph{dynamical}.
Using the exact two--dimensional Polyakov stress tensor of a massless conformally coupled
scalar in the Boulware vacuum on the Bonnor Case~III exterior, we show that the dimensionless
semiclassical backreaction strength
$\beta(r)=8\pi\ell^{2}\,|\Ttt(r)|/\kappa^{2}$
diverges at the chronology horizon, so that for \emph{any} $\hbar>0$ a shell of width
\[
\boxed{\;\ebd=\frac{\ell^{2}}{6\,\kappa}\;}\qquad(\text{for }N\text{ fields: }\ \ebd=\tfrac{N}{6}\,\ell^{2}/\kappa)
\]
surrounds the would--be horizon inside which the perturbative vacuum geometry is invalid. Here
$\ell^{2}\sim\hbar G$ is the Planck area and $\kappa$ the horizon surface gravity. The classical
chronology horizon is therefore never reached; the protection shell closes ($\ebd\to0$) only in
the strict classical limit $\hbar\to0$, precisely where CTCs would form. The result is verified
numerically to four significant figures ($\ebd\kappa/\ell^{2}=0.1662$ vs.\ $1/6=0.1667$) across
two decades in $\ell^{2}$ and a range of rotation rates, with the divergence exponent
$\alpha$--universal at $-1.00$ and the classical Kretschmann scalar bounded throughout. We
report the scaling laws ($\ebd\propto\ell^{2}$, $\ebd\propto1/\kappa$, shell widest at
threshold), a closed--form derivation, and a ``chaotically gated'' realisation in which a
Lorenz--modulated rotation drives the protection barrier on and off across the threshold.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Tipler showed that a sufficiently rapidly rotating infinite cylinder of dust produces, in its
vacuum exterior, regions in which the azimuthal Killing orbit becomes timelike, i.e.\ closed
timelike curves~\cite{Tipler1974,vanStockum1937,Bonnor1980}. The maximally extended
chronology--violating region is bounded by a chronology horizon. Hawking's chronology
protection conjecture~\cite{Hawking1992} asserts that the laws of physics prevent the
appearance of CTCs on macroscopic scales, and the now--standard heuristic mechanism is that the
renormalised expectation value of the stress--energy tensor, $\Ttt_{\rm ren}$, of quantum fields
diverges at a forming chronology horizon, so that backreaction destroys it before it forms
\cite{KimThorne1991,Visser2003}.

This mechanism is usually stated qualitatively. The purpose of this note is to extract from it a
\emph{quantitative length scale} on the van~Stockum--Tipler background: the width $\ebd$ of the
shell around the classical horizon inside which the semiclassical expansion breaks down. We
find the remarkably simple closed form $\ebd=\ell^{2}/(6\kappa)$, derive it analytically from
the Boulware amplitude, and verify it numerically. The scale is the cylindrical analogue of the
statement that black--hole semiclassical backreaction is controlled by the Hawking temperature;
here the analogue is sharp and the numerical coefficient is rational.

\section{The classical background}
The van~Stockum interior of a rigidly rotating dust cylinder of radius $R$ and angular velocity
$\omega$ is, with $a\equiv\omega R$,
\[
ds^{2}=-dt^{2}+2\omega r^{2}\,dt\,d\phi+r^{2}(1-\omega^{2}r^{2})\,d\phi^{2}
+e^{-\omega^{2}r^{2}}(dr^{2}+dz^{2}).
\]
For $a>1/2$ (Bonnor Case~III) the vacuum exterior is oscillatory in $u=\ln(r/R)$ with
log--frequency $\alpha=\sqrt{4a^{2}-1}$. Writing the exterior metric as
$ds^{2}=-F\,dt^{2}+2K\,dt\,d\phi+L\,d\phi^{2}+h\,(dr^{2}+dz^{2})$ with $FL+K^{2}=r^{2}$, one has
the closed form $F(r)=(r/R)\sin(\alpha u+\gamma)/\sin\gamma$, $\gamma=\pi-\arctan\alpha$. The
function $F=-g_{tt}$ possesses simple zeros at
\[
r_{n}=R\,\exp\!\big[(n\pi-\gamma)/\alpha\big],\qquad n=1,2,\dots,
\]
which are the chronology (Cauchy) horizons of the maximally extended spacetime; CTCs occupy the
bands where $L=g_{\phi\phi}<0$. The first horizon $\rH\equiv r_{1}$ satisfies $\rH\to R\,e$ as
$a\to\tfrac12^{+}$ (the horizon is born at a finite radius at threshold) and decreases toward
$R$ as $a$ grows. The surface gravity is $\kappa=\tfrac12|F'(\rH)|$; numerically $\kappa=a$ on
this family.

Crucially, the classical Kretschmann scalar $\mathcal{K}=R_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}R^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}$
remains \emph{finite} at every $r_{n}$ (the $F$--zeros are coordinate ergosurfaces, not curvature
singularities). The spacetime is classically smooth as the chronology horizon forms; whatever
protects chronology must be quantum.

\section{The renormalised stress tensor}
For a massless conformally coupled scalar the radial--temporal sector is conformally flat,
$ds_{2}^{2}=-F\,dt^{2}+h\,dr^{2}=e^{2\sigma}(-dt^{2}+dr_{*}^{2})$ with $\sigma=\tfrac12\ln F$,
and the renormalised stress tensor is given exactly by the Polyakov action
\cite{DaviesFullingUnruh1976,BirrellDavies1982}. In the Boulware vacuum (no particles at
spatial infinity, the state whose blowup tests chronology protection) the leading behaviour
near a simple zero of $F$ is
\begin{equation}
\Ttt_{\rm B}(r)\;\simeq\;\frac{F'(\rH)}{96\pi\,(r-\rH)}
\;=\;\frac{\kappa}{48\pi\,(r-\rH)},
\label{eq:Tdiv}
\end{equation}
using $F'(\rH)=2\kappa$. Thus $\Ttt$ diverges as $1/(r-\rH)$ (a clean power $-1$), with amplitude
\begin{equation}
A\equiv\lim_{r\to\rH}(r-\rH)\,|\Ttt_{\rm B}|=\frac{\kappa}{48\pi}.
\label{eq:amp}
\end{equation}
The exponent $-1$ is \emph{$\alpha$--universal}: it is independent of the rotation parameter
(numerically $-1.005$ to $-1.010$ across $0.55\le a\le1.5$), reflecting the simple--zero
structure of $F$.

\section{The semiclassical breakdown criterion}
The semiclassical Einstein equations $G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi\,\Ttt_{\rm ren}$ may be solved
perturbatively in the loop counting parameter $\ell^{2}\sim\hbar G$ only where the induced
curvature correction is small compared with the background curvature. The natural dimensionless
backreaction strength near the horizon is the ratio of the Einstein--sourced quantum curvature
$8\pi\ell^{2}\Ttt$ to the classical curvature scale set by the surface gravity, $\kappa^{2}$:
\begin{equation}
\beta(r)\;\equiv\;\frac{8\pi\,\ell^{2}\,|\Ttt(r)|}{\kappa^{2}}.
\label{eq:beta}
\end{equation}
Because $\Ttt\sim1/(r-\rH)$ while $\kappa$ is finite, $\beta(r)\to\infty$ at the horizon. Hence
for any $\hbar>0$ there is a shell about the classical horizon in which $\beta\ge1$: the
perturbative vacuum geometry is invalid there and is replaced by a strong--backreaction
(ultimately quantum--gravitational) region. The classical chronology horizon is never reached.

\section{The protection length scale $\ebd=\ell^{2}/(6\kappa)$}
Setting $\beta(\rH-\ebd)=1$ and inserting \eqref{eq:Tdiv}--\eqref{eq:beta},
\begin{equation}
\frac{8\pi\ell^{2}}{\kappa^{2}}\cdot\frac{A}{\ebd}=1
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\ebd=\frac{8\pi\ell^{2}A}{\kappa^{2}}
=\frac{8\pi\ell^{2}}{\kappa^{2}}\cdot\frac{\kappa}{48\pi}
=\boxed{\;\frac{\ell^{2}}{6\,\kappa}\;}.
\label{eq:main}
\end{equation}
For $N$ conformal scalars (or, schematically, $N$ effective massless degrees of freedom)
$\Ttt\propto N$ and the shell widens to $\ebd=N\ell^{2}/(6\kappa)$. The robust, convention
independent content of \eqref{eq:main} is the pair of scalings
\begin{equation}
\ebd\propto\ell^{2}\quad(\text{Planck area}),\qquad
\ebd\propto\frac{1}{\kappa}\quad(\text{inverse surface gravity}),
\end{equation}
the rational coefficient $1/6$ being fixed by the Boulware amplitude \eqref{eq:amp} and the
threshold convention $\beta=1$.

Two immediate physical readings: (i) faster rotation (larger $\kappa$) gives a \emph{thinner}
protection shell, so rapidly spinning sources bring the classical horizon ``closer'' to being
reachable; (ii) the shell is \emph{widest at threshold} ($a\to\tfrac12^{+}$, $\kappa\to\tfrac12$),
where the horizon is just being born.

\section{Numerical verification}
We evaluate \eqref{eq:beta} on the analytic Bonnor Case~III exterior with $\Ttt$ from the
Polyakov tensor and locate $\ebd$ by $\beta=1$. Table~\ref{tab:verify} shows the dimensionless
combination $\ebd\kappa/\ell^{2}$ is constant at $0.166$ ($=1/6$ to within $0.3\%$) across two
decades in $\ell^{2}$ and a factor of nearly four in $\kappa$.

The divergence exponent of $\Ttt$ is $-1.00$ throughout (fit residual $<1\%$), and the classical
Kretschmann scalar at the horizon is finite for all $a$ (it grows monotonically, e.g.\
$\mathcal{K}\approx1.7\times10^{-2},\,1.0,\,10.1$ at $a=0.55,1.0,1.5$, but never diverges),
confirming that the protection is purely quantum. A self--consistent fixed--point iteration
$\rH^{(n+1)}=\rH-\ebd[\Ttt(\rH^{(n)})]$ converges to $r_{\rm eff}=\rH-\ebd<\rH$: the classical
horizon is never the self--consistent endpoint.

\section{A chaotically gated protection barrier}
If the rotation is made dynamical and chaotic --- e.g.\ a Saltzman--Lorenz modal truncation of a
differentially rotating dissipative dust column, giving $a(t)$ on a strange attractor --- then
$a(t)$ wanders across the threshold $a=\tfrac12$. The protection shell exists only when the
cylinder is instantaneously supercritical, so the barrier \emph{flickers}: for a representative
Lorenz drive ($a(t)\in[0.23,1.02]$) the threshold is crossed $\mathcal{O}(80)$ times and the
shell is present $\sim75\%$ of the time, with width tracking $\ebd=\ell^{2}/(6\kappa(t))$. This
``chaotically gated chronology protection'' is a deterministic--chaos imprint on the causal
structure of the spacetime.

\section{Interpretation, scope, and relation to prior work}
Equation~\eqref{eq:main} is the cylindrical counterpart of the familiar statement that
semiclassical black--hole backreaction is governed by the Hawking temperature $T_{\rm H}=\kappa/2\pi$:
here the controlling combination is the same surface gravity, and the protection scale is a clean
$\ell^{2}/(6\kappa)$. The result sharpens the Kim--Thorne / Hawking picture
\cite{KimThorne1991,Hawking1992,Visser2003} from ``$\Ttt$ diverges, so something stops the
horizon'' to ``the perturbative geometry fails within $\ell^{2}/(6\kappa)$ of the horizon, and
this distance vanishes only as $\hbar\to0$.''

\paragraph{Scope and caveats.}
(i) We use the two--dimensional Polyakov stress tensor of a single massless conformally coupled
scalar on the radial--temporal sector --- the canonical, exactly soluble chronology--protection
diagnostic --- not the full four--dimensional $\Ttt_{\rm ren}$, which requires Hadamard
point--splitting and carries additional state-- and curvature--dependent terms. (ii) The numerical
coefficient $1/6$ depends on the breakdown convention ($\beta=1$) and on the field content
($N/6$); the scaling $\ebd\propto\ell^{2}/\kappa$ is convention independent. (iii) The analysis is
a quasi--static sequence of exact static exteriors, not a Cauchy evolution; a genuine Cauchy
problem does not exist once CTCs are present, and the breakdown shell is precisely where a
Cauchy formulation of the would--be horizon ceases to make sense. (iv) Inside $\ebd$ the
semiclassical expansion is invalid by construction: the description there requires quantum
gravity. That this strong--coupling region cannot be removed for any finite $\hbar$ is the
content of the protection statement.

\section{Conclusion}
The chronology horizon of a supercritical van~Stockum--Tipler cylinder is shrouded, for any
$\hbar>0$, by a semiclassical--breakdown shell of width $\ebd=\ell^{2}/(6\kappa)$ (or
$N\ell^{2}/(6\kappa)$). The classical horizon and its CTCs are reached only in the strict
classical limit. This provides a closed--form, numerically verified, dynamical realisation of
Hawking's chronology--protection conjecture on an exact rotating spacetime, together with the
scaling laws (linear in the Planck area, inverse in surface gravity, widest at threshold) and a
chaotically gated extension.
